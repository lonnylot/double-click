The night the fog missed the headland

By ten the horn had gone quiet. The keeper walked the east wall with a thermos and counted the intervals anyway, the way you check a lock you already know is shut.

The fog missed the headland entirely. It sat in a pale bank offshore, bright enough to read by, and left the rocks dry and ordinary under the lamp.

He wrote that down because it was the sort of thing that would sound invented later. A clear night is not a story. A clear night next to a wall of fog is a seam, and seams are where people look twice.

In the morning the bank was gone and the log had one extra line. He underlined it once, then went to make coffee.
